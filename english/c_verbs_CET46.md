### 1. calculate <a id="1-calculate"></a>

**Etymology:** From Latin calculatus, past participle of calculare 'to reckon, compute', from calculus 'small stone used in counting'.

**词源：** 源自拉丁语 calculatus，是 calculare 的过去分词，意为"计算、估算"，源自 calculus"用于计数的小石子"。

**Definitions:**

- EN: to determine mathematically; to reckon or estimate
- CN: 计算；估算；推测

**Part of Speech:** vt./vi.

**Usage Notes:**

- Calculate is often followed by a noun clause or wh-clause. — 常接名词从句或 wh-从句。
- Can be used transitively or intransitively. — 可及物或不及物使用。

**Common Collocations/Phrases:**

**General Collocations (English):**

- calculate the cost
- calculate the distance
- calculate precisely/accurately
- calculate the total
- calculate the area
- calculate the probability
- be calculated to do sth
- calculate the risk
- calculate statistically
- calculate the average

### 2. call <a id="2-call"></a>

**Etymology:** Old English ceallian 'to call, shout', from Old Norse kalla 'to cry out'.

**词源：** 古英语 ceallian"叫喊"，源自古诺尔斯语 kalla"大声呼喊"。

**Definitions:**

- EN: to cry out; to summon; to name; to telephone
- CN: 叫喊；召唤；称呼；打电话

**Part of Speech:** vt./vi.

**Usage Notes:**

- Call is one of the most versatile English verbs with many phrasal verb meanings. — 英语中最灵活的词之一，有大量短语动词含义。
- Can be used with an object complement: call sb sth. — 可接宾语补足语：call sb sth。

**Common Collocations/Phrases:**

**General Collocations (English):**

- call a meeting
- call the police
- call a taxi
- call sb's name
- call an ambulance
- call attention to
- call it a day
- call the shots
- call into question
- call a truce
